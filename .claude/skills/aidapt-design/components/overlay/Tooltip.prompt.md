Hover/focus tooltip for terse hints on icon buttons and truncated text.

```jsx
<Tooltip label="From > to — source to target" placement="top">
  <span>EN &gt; JA</span>
</Tooltip>
```

Placements: `top` (default) · `bottom` · `left` · `right`. Keep labels short; for rich content use a Popover/Modal. Deep Flow bubble, white text, appears on hover and focus.
